# Earlyric

**Practice your English listening with the songs and videos you like.**
Search a song, listen line by line, and type what you hear. Free, for Windows.

## Download

Get **`Earlyric-Setup-x.y.z.exe`** from the [latest release](https://github.com/cor3nt1nn/earlyric-releases/releases/latest) and run it.
Once installed, Earlyric updates itself.

The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC":
click **More info → Run anyway**.

## What's inside

- **4 modes**: full typing, fill the blanks, dictation, and "from memory" (type the line before you hear it)
- **Your ear**: what kind of words you miss (contractions, small words, word endings…), your typical mishearings
- **Meaning of a word**: click a word you found to see its translation and definitions
- **Goals**: daily challenges, achievements, XP and levels, song difficulty, survival mode (3 lives)
- **Multiplayer**: challenge a friend with a code, take turns on one computer, or a live duel between two computers
- English or French interface, light or dark theme

# Earlyric (français)

**Progresse en compréhension orale de l'anglais avec les chansons et vidéos que tu aimes.**
Cherche une chanson, écoute ligne par ligne, et tape ce que tu entends. Gratuit, pour Windows.

Télécharge **`Earlyric-Setup-x.y.z.exe`** dans la [dernière version](https://github.com/cor3nt1nn/earlyric-releases/releases/latest) et lance-le.
Une fois installé, Earlyric se met à jour tout seul.

L'installeur n'est pas encore signé : si Windows affiche « Windows a protégé votre ordinateur »,
clique sur **Informations complémentaires → Exécuter quand même**.

*This repository only hosts the installers; the source code is private.*
